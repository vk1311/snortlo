# Caption style (memes + Congress Moments)

The caption on top is what makes people laugh, tag a friend and share. A literal description of what's on screen
("when the timer hits zero") is NOT a joke. Owner feedback, Oct 8: "be funnier, push limits, be edgy, be relatable".

**Formula:** take what's happening on screen and map it onto a CLASSIC RELATIONSHIP DYNAMIC everyone has lived
(owner's pick, Oct 8): wife vs husband, siblings, mom/dad vs kid, besties, the friend group. Work, school, exes and money are second choice.
Use the classic "Me: / Her:", "Me vs. my wife", "My brother when…", "Mom when…", "My bestie when…" setups so it reads in 2 seconds.

Good:
- "Me explaining why I got home at 4 AM|vs. my wife, 10 seconds in:" (a politician cut off mid-sentence)
- "Bro got muted like a 12-year-old|in an Xbox lobby" (the same clip)
- "Me 45 minutes into explaining|why my ex is actually the problem:" (someone ranting, then cut off)
- "My mom when I say 'one more episode'|at 3 AM:" (someone banging a gavel)
- "The group chat when I finally|share my 'small' opinion:" (chaos and shouting)

Bad: "Congress has a MUTE button. Why doesn't Thanksgiving dinner?" (owner didn't get it: too clever, needs a second read), "When you're about to make your best point and the timer hits 0" (literal, nobody relates to a timer),
"Funny Congress moment" (a description, not a joke).

Rules:
- Edgy = roasting everyday life and ourselves, with a little attitude. It never means slurs, sexual jokes, punching down
  (race, religion, disability, gender, body), jokes about tragedies or violence, or mocking someone's political views.
- Politics clips: the joke is about the SITUATION (cut off, ignored, chaos), never the person's party or position.
  Never imply a real person said or did something they didn't.
- Short: two lines max, about 10–14 words. Read it out loud; if it doesn't land in 2 seconds, rewrite it.
- Write 5 options, pick the one you'd actually send to a friend.
- Emojis go in the post caption, not the on-screen text (the video font can't draw them).
