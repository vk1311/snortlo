Shops in Nova Scotia open almost as fast as they close, and Halifax retail space is scarce. So who's signing the leases, and why do some windows stay empty for years? We look at Statistics Canada's openings and closures, how gross, net and triple net leases really work, Halifax's commercial tax rate, who's opening (Dollarama, franchise owners, quick service food), the cap rate math that keeps some storefronts empty, the Competition Bureau's grocery property controls case, where everyone else went, and five things to check before you sign a shop lease.
General information as of November 2026. This isn't legal or tax advice.
