What psychological safety at work really means, where the idea came from, and what a manager can actually do about workplace conflict.
The research: Amy Edmondson's 1996 hospital study and 1999 team study, and Google's research on 180 teams.
In Canada: the voluntary National Standard for Psychological Health and Safety in the Workplace, and Nova Scotia's harassment prevention policy rules (in force since Sept 1, 2025). In the news: the 2026 living wage for Nova Scotia.
Hosts and guests on this show are AI voices. Facts are sourced below. Not legal advice.
