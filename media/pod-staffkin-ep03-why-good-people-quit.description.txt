Why good employees quit, the 13 signs researchers found in the months before they go, and what a manager can actually do: stay conversations, specific recognition and room to grow.
The history: the Hawthorne studies (1924-1933) and what later economists found in the original data, plus the Great Resignation and the MIT Sloan research on toxic culture.
In the news: Canada's and Nova Scotia's October 2026 jobs numbers. Nova Scotia notice rules when someone quits, and the overtime change coming April 1, 2027.
Hosts and guests on this show are AI voices. Facts are sourced below. Not legal advice.
