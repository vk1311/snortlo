The toxic boss: how to tell a tough boss from an abusive one, what 25 years of research on abusive supervision found, and what employees, coworkers and owners can actually do.
The research: Tepper (2000), a 119-sample review, the trickle-down effect, the family spillover study, Gallup on managers, and a Swedish heart study.
In Nova Scotia: harassment prevention policy rules in force since Sept 1, 2025, plus the federal Canada Labour Code rules. In the news: the 2026 living wage and a rejected tentative deal.
