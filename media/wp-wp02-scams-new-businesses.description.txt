Why brand new Canadian businesses get fake invoices, fake renewal notices, fake C.R.A. calls and fake grant offers, how each scam works, and the five free habits that stop almost all of them.
Facts are from the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre, the Competition Bureau of Canada, the CRA and the Government of Nova Scotia. Information is as of November 2026. This isn't legal or tax advice.
