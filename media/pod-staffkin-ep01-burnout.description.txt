Burnout at work: the three signs, where the word came from (Freudenberger in the 1970s, Maslach's research, the WHO's 2019 ICD-11 listing), and what a small-business owner or manager can actually do about it.
Three real-world situations: the reliable shift lead who starts slipping, the hero who never takes vacation, and the owner who burns out.
The six areas of worklife, how to have the conversation, what not to say, Nova Scotia vacation and sick leave rules, and when to point someone to an EAP or professional help.
Hosts and guests on this show are AI voices. Facts are sourced below. Not medical advice. Not legal advice.
If you or someone you know is in crisis in Canada, call or text 9-8-8. In Nova Scotia, the Provincial Mental Health and Addictions Crisis Line is 1-888-429-8167 (24/7). If safety is at risk, call 9-1-1.
